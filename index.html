The meaning behind our name

Crazy About the Beach!

“Makaloka: Crazy About the Beach!”

Have you ever experienced a place so beautiful that it makes you fall in love at first sight? A place where the sound of the waves, the cool sea breeze, and the beauty of nature make you forget your worries and simply enjoy life? That is the inspiration behind the name Makaloka Beach Resort.

The name Makaloka was inspired by the Filipino word “nakakaloka,” an expression commonly used to describe something so amazing, exciting, or overwhelming that it leaves you speechless. For us, it represents being absolutely crazy about the beach!

The idea behind the name is simple: when people discover the beauty of the sea, feel the refreshing breeze, and spend unforgettable moments with family and friends, they experience a kind of happiness that makes them want to come back again and again.

Located in Tiwasan, Catarman, Liloan, Cebu, Makaloka Beach Resort has a name that reflects the joy, excitement, and relaxation a beach getaway can bring. It represents falling in love with the ocean, enjoying life's simple pleasures, and creating memories worth keeping.

At Makaloka, every visit is an opportunity to laugh a little louder, relax a little longer, and make beautiful memories with the people who matter most.

Because sometimes, the best kind of nakakaloka is being completely crazy about the beach!
